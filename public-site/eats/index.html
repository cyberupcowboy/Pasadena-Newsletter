How the Current picks Local Eats

Hot right now looks for current buzz, new energy, seasonal pull and places drawing unusual local attention. Local favorites recognizes durable neighborhood loyalty. These are editorial labels—not star ratings, paid placement or claims that one restaurant is objectively “best.” Sponsored businesses remain clearly labeled in the directory and do not receive an Eats label because they paid.
