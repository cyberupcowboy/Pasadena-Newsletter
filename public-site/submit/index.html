Community Voices

Your neighborhood. Your reporting. Your point of view.

Send us something Pasadena should know: a reported story, neighborhood notice, opinion, event, yard sale, lost/found item, business listing, photo story or tip. Nothing submitted here goes straight onto the site.

What happens after you submit
  1. Your original submission is saved exactly as you wrote it.
  2. Our AI copy desk may correct grammar, spelling, repetition and organization, fact-check public claims and flag harmful language. It is instructed not to invent facts or change your viewpoint.
  3. A human editor compares the original with the edited version and decides whether to publish it.
