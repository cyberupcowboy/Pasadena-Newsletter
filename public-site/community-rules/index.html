Community discussion

Disagree vigorously. Treat people like neighbors.

The Current is not trying to make Pasadena politically uniform. Strong opinions, criticism of public officials and unpopular viewpoints are welcome. The line is behavior that makes discussion unsafe, personally destructive or impossible.

What belongs here

Allowed

Political disagreement

Support or oppose candidates, parties, policies, school decisions, zoning proposals or public officials. Explain why.

Allowed

Sharp criticism

You can say a public decision was foolish, wasteful or unfair. Criticize conduct and ideas without trying to intimidate the person.

Allowed

Firsthand experience

Share what you personally saw or experienced. Be clear about what you know directly versus what you heard from someone else.

What gets held or removed

Not allowed

Threats & intimidation

Threats of violence, encouragement of harm, stalking or attempts to frighten someone out of participating.

Not allowed

Doxxing & private information

Private addresses, phone numbers, personal schedules or other information shared to expose or target someone.

Not allowed

Targeted harassment or slurs

Repeated personal abuse, discriminatory slurs or comments whose main purpose is humiliating another participant.

Needs evidence

Serious factual accusations

Claims that someone committed a crime, stole money, abused a child or similar serious allegations need reliable evidence and attribution—not rumor presented as fact.

How moderation works: comments are screened for threats, harassment, doxxing, slurs and risky factual accusations. Civil comments can clear automatically; uncertain or sensitive comments go to a human editor. A political opinion is not a moderation violation by itself.
Think we got it wrong? Use the Report control on a comment or the Report an Error control on a story. Human editors can override automated moderation decisions.
